## src/game

- `src/game/classic.ts`: Classic-grid engine: create, legal moves, win/misère evaluation, bonus placements, and apply/clone helpers.
- `src/game/connect.ts`: Connect Four engine for a 7×6 board with gravity drops, four-in-a-row checks, and validation.
- `src/game/engine.ts`: Dispatches clone, legal-move, validate, and apply helpers across classic, connect, overflow, and ultimate kinds.
- `src/game/overflow.ts`: 5×5 FIFO overflow mode that keeps at most three marks per side and reuses classic evaluation.
- `src/game/types.ts`: Shared types for sides, actions, validation, and classic/connect/overflow/ultimate game states.
- `src/game/ultimate.ts`: Ultimate tic-tac-toe rules for nine boards, active-realm routing, and meta-board winners.

## src/game/ai

- `src/game/ai/lordO.ts`: After two draws, Lord O throws games via gift moves and bored thinking-line copy.
- `src/game/ai/minimax.ts`: Alpha-beta minimax, heuristics, legal-action conversion, and best-move selection for AI.
- `src/game/ai/strategies.ts`: Routes lord AI to perfect, weak, misère-fool, connect, or bored-throwing strategies.

## src/hooks

- `src/hooks/useBattle.ts`: Battle reducer hook that applies human moves and delayed lord AI turns with optional bored-O flavor.
